# IM unit alignment — November 17, 2026

The classroom uses Imagine Learning for elementary mathematics and Illustrative
Mathematics through Desmos for Grade 6, as specified by the teacher. The website
now connects each of its 70 current skills to an IM unit. This is a teaching
crosswalk; it does not claim that website practice replaces the publisher's
lessons, that every topic in a unit is taught, or that the student's school has
finished a unit.

Each skill has one primary home so a skill revisited in several weeks is counted
once. Related units appear as supporting connections. The mapping is the site's
editorial alignment based on the skill's learning target and the cited unit
contents; it is not an official publisher certification.

Fraction comparison (`F-COMP`) is marked as an earlier-grade prerequisite used
in 5.6, not a newly introduced Grade 5 concept. `G-PERIM` uses a geometry setting
but targets writing an equation, so its primary home is 6.6. `G-VOL` concerns
fractional side lengths and belongs to 6.4; whole-number volume remains in 5.1.
Axis reflection in `N-REFLECT` means opposite coordinates in 6.7 and is not
classified as a Grade 8 transformations course. `E-LIKE` connects 6.6 to 7.6.

Unit 5.2 has supporting connections through fractions-as-quotients, fraction
multiplication and numerical expressions. It is not labeled absent merely
because those shared skills have a different primary home. The review/capstone
units and 7.7 remain listed with no primary course skills rather than inventing
coverage for them.

## What coverage means

The course map reports counts of **this website's skills** that have received
saved online answers and counts currently marked mastered by the existing
numerical progress system. A related connection does not add another practiced
skill. Merely being available, appearing in a week, or having an AI suggestion
never creates mastery. The map does not calculate a percentage of the complete
IM curriculum or declare an IM unit complete. Units with no primary site skills
remain visible as such.

The new `server/im-alignment.mjs` is an independent display and metadata overlay.
It does not modify the checked question generators, the private no-repeat bank,
the course schedule, stored answers or grading rules. Tests cover all 70 skills,
important grade boundaries, duplicate week entries, missing progress, prerequisite
labels and the distinction between course-skill counts and whole-unit coverage.

The linked IM materials identify their authors and Creative Commons licenses.
The site uses names and source links for reference, without IM logos or claims
of affiliation or endorsement.
